What We Offer

One platform, every safety workflow

Built around a real-time CCTV pipeline, the system ties together identity, safety, health, and payroll so nothing falls through the cracks.

CCTV & Face Recognition

Live camera feeds identify workers automatically at entry points, replacing manual sign-in sheets with instant, accurate recognition.

Violation Detection

Missing helmets, vests, and unsafe zones are flagged the moment they happen, tagged by severity and routed to supervisors instantly.

Health & Safety Alerts

Fatigue and distress signals trigger critical alerts, with response times logged so nothing goes unnoticed on-site.

Attendance Tracking

Check-in and check-out times, working hours, and lateness are calculated automatically from recognition events — no paperwork.

Salary & Payroll

Wages are computed from real attendance data, with violation fines deducted automatically before each pay period is processed.

Worker Management

A central directory of every worker's profile, department, wage type, and photo — searchable and always up to date.

About Us

Built as a final year project, engineered like a product

The Intelligent Surveillance System for Enhanced Safety at Construction Sites is a Final Year Project aimed at solving a very real problem: manual attendance registers, missed PPE violations, and delayed health emergencies on active construction sites.

By combining live CCTV feeds, face recognition, and an automated backend, the system turns raw camera footage into attendance records, safety alerts, and payroll figures — all without a supervisor needing to watch every screen at once.
